Hoppin App Redesign

Redesigning a complex mobility app into a clearer, more intuitive experience.

Role: UX/UI design + front-end Tools: Figma, HTML, CSS Type: Case study

Overview

Hoppin is a mobility platform that combines transport options like buses, trains, shared bikes, and other shared services in one app. The goal of this project was to redesign the mobile experience so users could plan trips faster, understand the interface more easily, and feel more confident using the service.

Process

Challenge

The original app felt cluttered and unclear, with too many options competing for attention. It was difficult to understand what the app did at a glance, and the booking flow needed to be simpler.

Approach

I started by reviewing the existing app and identifying pain points through testing and competitor research. From there, I focused on simplifying the navigation, improving hierarchy, and making the main trip-planning flow easier to follow.

Build

I designed wireframes and high-fidelity screens in Figma, then refined the visual system with clean typography, strong contrast, and a more approachable interface. I also considered accessibility and clarity across key interaction states.

Outcome

The redesign creates a smoother experience with clearer entry points, better structure, and a more trustworthy feel. It shows my ability to turn a messy interface into a more focused product experience.
